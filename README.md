## Investigation Methodology

Reports generally follow this analytical workflow:

```text
Intelligence Question
        ↓
Scope
        ↓
Collection
        ↓
Verification
        ↓
Correlation
        ↓
Analysis
        ↓
Assessment
        ↓
Confidence & Limitations
```

Where possible, reports distinguish clearly between:

**Evidence** — information directly supported by available sources.

**Analysis** — interpretation and correlation of collected information.

**Assessment** — the final analytical judgment based on the available evidence.

Assessments include an explicit confidence level where appropriate:

* High Confidence
* Moderate Confidence
* Low Confidence

## Ethics and Scope

All investigations published in this repository are based exclusively on publicly available or intentionally provided information.

The objective of this repository is educational and professional development.

Investigations are designed to avoid unnecessary collection or publication of personal information. Where appropriate, examples may use simulated data, public organizations, historical cases, infrastructure, or intentionally created training material.

No attempt is made to bypass authentication, access controls, or technical restrictions protecting non-public information.

## Reproducibility

Whenever practical, reports document:

* sources consulted;
* date of access;
* search methodology;
* analytical steps;
* relevant tools;
* limitations;
* confidence in the resulting assessment.

This is intended to make the analytical process understandable and, where possible, independently reproducible.
